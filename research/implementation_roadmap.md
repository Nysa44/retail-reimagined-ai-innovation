# 90-day implementation roadmap

## Days 1–15: Define and govern
- Select one category, customer segment and journey.
- Document baseline metrics, data sources, owners and consent requirements.
- Define human override, escalation and rollback criteria.

## Days 16–35: Prepare and prototype
- Audit catalogue quality, inventory freshness and event completeness.
- Establish baseline recommendation and forecast evaluation.
- Add monitoring, access controls and feedback capture.

## Days 36–60: Controlled pilot
- Launch to a limited cohort with a holdout where feasible.
- Track outcome and guardrail metrics.
- Review sampled recommendations, forecast errors and customer feedback.

## Days 61–75: Evaluate
- Compare pilot and holdout with appropriate statistical methods.
- Separate AI effects from seasonality, promotions and assortment changes.
- Review qualitative feedback and operational workload.

## Days 76–90: Decide
- Scale, iterate or stop based on evidence.
- Confirm ongoing data/model maintenance and total cost of ownership.
- Train operating teams and document accountability.
