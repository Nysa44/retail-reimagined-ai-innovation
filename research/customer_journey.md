# Connected retail customer journey

| Stage | Customer need | AI-enabled moment | Data required | Human / fallback |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Discover | Find suitable products quickly | Personalised product ranking | Product attributes and consented interactions | Generic browse and preference reset |
| Evaluate | Compare products and availability | Relevant comparisons grounded in catalogue | Product catalogue and inventory freshness | Store associate or product detail page |
| Purchase | Choose a reliable fulfilment promise | Demand and inventory-aware promise | Basket, stock, delivery slots | Alternative slot or pickup |
| Fulfil | Know where order is and when it arrives | Proactive exception alerts | Order events and carrier updates | Human service escalation |
| Return / support | Resolve without repeating context | Agent summary and policy-grounded next action | Order history and return policy | Human agent handoff |
| Re-engage | Receive useful, not intrusive, follow-up | Relevant replenishment or complementary products | Consent and purchase context | Opt-out and frequency controls |

When an item is unavailable or ETA slips, communicate early, explain options and let the customer choose an alternative, pickup, revised promise or cancellation.
